Delete Your CalorieLens Account

CalorieLens is operated by Donggi Yang. This page explains how to delete your CalorieLens account and the data linked to it. It applies to the iOS and Android apps, which use the same account.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open CalorieLens and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm the deletion.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What is deleted

Deleting your account permanently deletes the account and all data linked to it:

  • Your profile (email, name, picture) and body metrics
  • Meal entries and macros, and your meal photos
  • Weight log entries and saved meals
  • Supplements, supplement intake logs, and reminders
  • Dietary preferences
  • Friend connections and challenge participation
  • Your AI coaching note

Data that CalorieLens wrote to Apple Health or Android Health Connect on your device stays on your device. Remove it there with the Health app or the Health Connect settings.

Delete your account without the app

If you cannot open the app, email xronace@gmail.com from the address linked to your CalorieLens account and ask us to delete your account.
