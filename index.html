Getting Started

  1. Sign in Open CalorieLens and tap Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google. No password to create — you're in within seconds.
  2. Set up your goal A short first-run walkthrough asks for your height, weight, age, activity level and goal, then calculates a personalised daily calorie target using the Mifflin-St Jeor formula. Prefer to set it yourself? Just tap Skip.
  3. Log your meal On the Analyze tab, take a photo or pick one from your library — or skip the photo entirely and just type what you ate (e.g. “large bowl of ramen”). Either way the AI does the rest.
  4. Review, adjust & save The AI estimates calories, protein, carbs and fat in a few seconds and gives the meal a health grade (A–D). Tap a portion size (½×–3×) to scale it, set the “Eaten At” time if you're logging a past meal, then tap Save & Done.

Feature Guides

📸

Analyze a meal

Photograph your plate from directly above with everything in frame for the best estimate. Add a meal hint for context, then review the calorie and macro breakdown. Not quite right? Edit the description and re-analyze, or adjust the numbers before saving.

📝

Log without a photo New

Can't snap a picture? On the Analyze tab, just type what you ate — like “a bowl of ramen” — and tap Analyze from description. The AI estimates calories and macros from your words alone, no camera needed.

🎚️

Adjustable portions New

Ate more or less than a standard serving? On any analysis result, tap a portion size — ½×, 1×, 1½×, 2× or 3× — and the calories, macros and ingredient breakdown all scale instantly. What you save reflects what you actually ate.

🏠

Your day at a glance New

The Analyze tab now opens with a “Today” card: how many calories you've had, how many are left against your goal, and how many AI analyses you have remaining — all before you log a thing.

🥦

Dietary preferences New

Set your diet (vegetarian, vegan, pescatarian, keto, paleo, halal, kosher, low-carb) and any allergies in Settings → Edit Profile. The AI takes them into account and flags conflicts right in the meal description — so you're warned if a dish doesn't fit.

🏆

Achievements New

See your lifetime stats in one place: total meals logged, distinct days tracked, current and longest streaks, meal-grade breakdown, and how long you've been a member.

🧑‍🎨

Profile, avatar & goal weight New

Make it yours in Settings → Edit Profile: pick an emoji avatar and color, update your name and body metrics, and set a goal weight to track toward. Changing your metrics re-calculates your daily calorie goal automatically.

🔖

Saved meals & one-tap re-logging

Eat the same things often? Save any analyzed meal as a template, then re-log it in a single tap from the bookmark icon in the Analyze tab — no new photo or analysis needed, and it doesn't use your daily quota.

🗓️

History, rings & calendar

The History tab shows Apple-Watch-style rings for today's calories and macros against your goals, plus a monthly calendar with a dot on every day you logged. Tap any day to filter the list to just those meals.

📈

Trends & weekly summary

See your calorie and macro intake charted over the last 7 or 30 days. Each week you get a summary with averages, goal-hit days and streak info — shareable as an image, and delivered as a Sunday digest if notifications are on.

💬

AI weekly coaching

Open the weekly summary to get a short, personalised coaching note generated from your own week — days logged, average calories, macro balance, goal-hit days and streak — with encouragement and one concrete tip for the week ahead.

🔥

Streaks

Log at least one meal a day to build your streak — the app tracks your current and longest runs. An optional 8 PM reminder nudges you on days you haven't logged yet, so you don't break the chain.

🤝

Friends & challenges

Add friends with their 8-character invite code (in Settings → Plan) to compare streaks and stay accountable. Start group challenges — log-streak, daily-calorie-goal or consecutive-day goals — and track everyone's progress in Settings → Friends.

📱

Home Screen widget

Add the CalorieLens widget for an at-a-glance view of today's calories, your remaining analyses and your current streak — without opening the app. Long-press the Home Screen, tap +, search “CalorieLens”, and choose the small or medium size.

⚖️

Weight & body metrics

Log your weight over time and watch it charted in Settings → Weight. Your body metrics also power the personalised calorie goal, and weight entries can sync to Apple Health.

🥗

Macros & goals

Choose which nutrients to track (protein, carbs, fat) and set a daily target for each in Settings → Nutrition Tracking. Your rings and charts update to match what matters to you.

🔔

Reminders

Enable optional notifications in Settings → Notifications: a daily meal-logging reminder at a time you pick, a streak-at-risk nudge, and the Sunday weekly-summary digest.

🍎

Apple Health sync

Turn on Settings → Health Sync to write your calories, protein, carbs, fat and weight to Apple Health as you log them, keeping all your wellness data in one place. CalorieLens only writes data — it never reads from Health.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sign in?

CalorieLens supports Sign in with Google and Sign in with Apple. Tap either button on the login screen and follow the on-screen steps. No password is created — authentication is handled entirely by Google or Apple.

How many meal analyses do I get per day?

Free plan: 3 analyses per day, plus up to +2 more by watching a short rewarded ad (maximum 5/day).
Standard plan ($3.99/month): 15 analyses per day with no ads.

Your quota resets at midnight in your local time zone each day.

Do saved meals and re-logging use my daily quota?

No. Your daily limit applies only to AI photo analysis. Re-logging a saved meal, or editing an existing entry, records it instantly and does not use one of your daily analyses — so meals you eat often are effectively free to log.

How do friends and challenges work?

Every account has a unique 8-character invite code in Settings → Plan. Share it with a friend and add each other to compare current and longest streaks. From Settings → Friends you can also start a challenge — a shared goal like logging every day, hitting your calorie target, or building a consecutive-day streak — and track everyone's progress together.

What is AI Weekly Coaching?

Once a week, CalorieLens generates a short, personalised note from your own data — days logged, average calories, macro balance, goal-hit days and streak — with encouragement and one practical tip. It's created from your weekly stats only and refreshes once per week; you'll find it in the weekly summary.

What if the calorie estimate seems wrong?

The AI estimates calories from the photo, so accuracy depends on lighting, angle, and how clearly all portions are visible. For best results:

  • Photograph the meal from directly above
  • Include a reference object (fork, plate edge) so portions can be judged
  • Add a meal description (e.g. "chicken caesar salad, restaurant portion") to give the AI extra context

You can edit the description and re-analyze, or manually adjust before saving.

How do I save a meal entry?

After the analysis completes, review the result and tap Save & Done. You can also set a custom "Eaten At" time if you're logging a past meal before saving.

How do I sync with Apple Health?

Go to Settings → Health Sync and enable the toggle. CalorieLens will write nutrition data (calories, protein, carbs, fat) and weight entries to Apple Health whenever you save them. The app does not read any data from Health.

How do I get more daily analyses for free?

When you reach your daily limit on the Free plan, you can watch a short rewarded ad to get +1 more analysis — up to 2 extra per day (5 total). For more, upgrade to Standard for 15 analyses per day with no ads.

How do I upgrade to Standard?

Go to Settings → Plan → Upgrade to Standard. The subscription is $3.99/month, billed through the App Store. You can cancel at any time from the App Store subscription settings.

How do I delete my account and data?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all associated data (entries, weight logs, body metrics, streaks). Deletion is immediate and irreversible.

You can also delete individual entries from the History tab by long-pressing a row or tapping into an entry and using the trash icon.

The app isn't connecting to the server. What should I do?

Check that you have an active internet connection. If the issue persists:

  • Force-quit and relaunch the app
  • Try toggling Airplane Mode off and on
  • If you see a sign-in error, try signing out and back in from Settings

If none of these help, contact support and include your device model and iOS version.
